## PROSPECTING PROTOCOL

### Daily Target: 20 qualified new prospects added to Lead Tracker

### Where to Find Them

**For Tier 1 — Brand Storytelling:**
- LinkedIn search: `"founder" + "raising" + [sector]` — filter to Africa/Nigeria
- LinkedIn search: `"CEO" + "building" + [sector]` — look for sparse or outdated profiles
- Accelerator alumni pages: Founders Factory Africa, YC Africa, Tony Elumelu Foundation, Catalyst Fund
- Look for: founders posting about fundraising, new ventures, or attending investor events — who clearly lack narrative polish in their bio or posts

**For Tier 1 — Automation:**
- LinkedIn: Operations managers, COOs, founders at 5-30 person companies
- Facebook Ads Library: Search for growing African SMBs running ads — if they're spending on ads, they're scaling and hitting process pain
- LinkedIn search: `"operations" + "agency" + Nigeria` or `"e-commerce" + "logistics" + Africa`
- Look for: anyone posting about hiring for admin roles, managing a growing team, or complaining about manual processes

**For Tier 2 — Content Strategy:**
- Instagram: Brands with 1,000–50,000 followers posting irregularly (more than 5 days between posts, or no clear content theme)
- LinkedIn: Founders with 500–10,000 connections who post 1x per month or less
- Accelerator alumni posting inconsistently — they have the credibility but not the strategy
- Look for: active profiles with real substance behind them, but content that's scattered or low-engagement

**For Tier 3 — Pitch Decks (lower volume, higher quality):**
- LinkedIn: Founders who posted about attending investor events, applying to accelerators, or preparing to raise
- Tony Elumelu Foundation fellows, YC Africa cohort members, Founders Factory alumni
- Search: `"pre-seed" + "raising" + Africa` or `"seed round" + "founder" + Nigeria`
- Only add Tier 3 prospects if they show clear fundraising intent — don't add speculatively

### How to Qualify a Prospect (Before Adding to Tracker)

Ask: Does this person have a clear pain that matches one of PLM's services?
- Do they have a weak/absent narrative but real traction? → Tier 1 Brand Storytelling
- Is their company scaling and likely hitting manual process bottlenecks? → Tier 1 Automation
- Do they have a following but inconsistent or weak content? → Tier 2 Content Strategy
- Are they actively preparing to raise from VCs/DFIs? → Tier 3 Pitch Deck

If you can't clearly answer YES to one of those — don't add them. Quality over quantity.

## WARM-UP PROTOCOL

**This must happen 24-48 hours before any DM is sent.**

1. Find the prospect's last 3-5 posts on LinkedIn (or Instagram if applicable)
2. **Like 2-3 posts** — not all at once. Space them across the day.
3. Leave **ONE specific, thoughtful comment** on their most recent relevant post
   - The comment must reference something specific in the post — not generic praise
   - Good: "This is a sharp observation — the same thing cost one of my clients 6 months of runway. What prompted you to write this now?"
   - Bad: "Great post! Really insightful."
4. Note in the Lead Tracker under Notes: "Warmed up [date]"
5. Set Follow-up Date to tomorrow (24 hours later) or the day after (48 hours)

**Do not skip this step.** Cold DMs with no prior interaction have a much lower reply rate.

## DM EXECUTION

### Daily Volume: 20 DMs

**Before writing each DM:**
1. Open their LinkedIn profile and read their bio, About section, and last 5 posts
2. Find one specific observation to personalise the message
3. Select the most relevant script from dm-scripts.md
4. Rewrite the opening line to be specific to them — never copy verbatim
5. Keep the message under 300 characters for the opening DM on LinkedIn

**After sending:**
- Update Lead Tracker: Status → **Cold**, Last Contact Date → today, Follow-up Date → 3 days from today
- Log in Notes: which script angle you used (e.g. "T1A-2 Fundraising Prep angle")

**Never:**
- Send the same opening line twice in the same week
- Send more than one DM per day to the same person
- DM someone who hasn't been warmed up
- Copy-paste a script word-for-word

## REPLY HANDLING GUIDE

**Check DMs: morning (9am) and afternoon (3pm) every day**

### If they reply positively ("interested", "tell me more", "how does this work"):
1. **Flag Seun within 1 hour** via WhatsApp: "Hot lead: [Name] at [Company] replied positively to the [service] DM. Here's what they said: [paste reply]"
2. Send a reply that: expands on the specific value in 2-3 sentences + ends with a clear CTA to book a call
3. Include Seun's booking link: https://calendly.com/seun-leadingman/client-discovery-plm
4. Update status to **Hot** in Lead Tracker
5. Set follow-up date to 2 days later in case they don't book

### If they ask "what's the price?":
- Give the price range directly. No dancing.
- Example: "The Founder Story Sprint is $1,200. It's a 48-hour project — you'd have the full document by end of week. Would it make sense to jump on a quick 15-min call to see if it's a fit for where you are right now?"
- If they say yes to the call → **Hot**, flag Seun
- If they say "let me think about it" → **Warm**, follow up in 5 days

### If they say "not right now" or "maybe later":
- Reply: "Understood — no pressure at all. I'll check back in a month when timing might be better."
- Update status to **Warm**
- Set follow-up date to 30 days from today
- Do NOT follow up before that date

### If they raise an objection (price too high, wrong timing, wrong service):
- Do NOT negotiate price or make promises about scope
- Reply: "That makes sense — can I ask what would need to be different for this to be a fit?"
- If they respond with a specific condition → **Flag Seun immediately** with full context
- Seun handles all objection conversations from this point

### If no reply after 3 days:
- Send **Follow-up 1** (see dm-scripts.md — follow-up scripts section)
- Use a new angle — don't repeat the original message
- Update Notes: "Follow-up 1 sent [date]"

### If no reply after 4 more days (7 days total):
- Send **Final message** (see dm-scripts.md)
- Update status to **Cold**
- Set no further follow-up date
- Do not send a 4th message to any prospect

### If they say no clearly:
- Reply graciously: "Totally fair — thanks for letting me know. I'll leave you to it."
- Update status to **Cold**
- No further contact

- **Post anything to Seun's LinkedIn** without his explicit approval
