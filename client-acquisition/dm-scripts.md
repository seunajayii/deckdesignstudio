## HOW TO USE THESE SCRIPTS

These are frameworks — not copy-paste templates. Lois must personalise every single message.

**Before sending any DM:**
1. Read the prospect's last 5 posts
2. Look at their bio, company page, and recent activity
3. Find ONE specific observation (a post they wrote, a milestone they shared, something on their website)
4. Insert that observation into the `[personalised observation]` placeholder
5. Never send the same opening line twice in the same week

**Warm-up rule:** Like 2-3 of their posts and leave ONE specific, thoughtful comment. Wait 24-48 hours. Then send the DM.

**Character limit:** Keep DMs under 300 characters on LinkedIn (first message). Save the detail for the follow-up.

## TIER 1B — ONE PROCESS AUTOMATED
**Service:** Identify one broken manual process and automate it using no-code tools (n8n, Make, Zapier, Airtable, etc.)
**Price:** $1,500 – $2,500
**Best for:** SMBs, agencies, e-commerce brands, any business with 5-30 staff doing repetitive manual work

---

### Script T1B-1: The Scaling Pain
*Angle: Their growth is creating operational debt*

> [Name], scaling [company] the way you are usually means the manual processes that worked at 5 people start breaking at 15. The ops debt catches up quietly. I find one process that's costing the most time and automate it in under a week — no bloated system builds, just one thing fixed cleanly. Would it be worth a quick look at where the biggest leak is?

**When to use:** Prospect has posted about hiring, team growth, or scaling challenges. Their company appears to be in a growth phase (new hires, new office, new funding announcement).

---

### Script T1B-2: The Ops Efficiency Angle
*Angle: Targeted at operations managers or COOs, not just founders*

> [Name], ops leads at companies your size usually have a shortlist of processes they know are broken but haven't had time to fix. I specialise in finding that one thing and automating it cleanly — under a week, using no-code tools. No vendor lock-in, no long implementation cycle. Just one painful process gone. What's the one thing your team does manually that you wish wasn't manual?

**When to use:** Prospect is an operations director, COO, or head of ops at a mid-size company. They're not the founder — they're the person actually managing the broken processes.

---

### Script T1B-3: The Revenue Leakage
*Angle: Manual processes = delayed invoicing, slow follow-ups, missed revenue*

> [Name], I was looking at how [company] operates and something jumped out — at your scale, the manual process gaps aren't just inefficiency, they're revenue leakage. Delayed invoices, slow client onboarding, missed follow-ups — these translate directly to money left on the table. I fix one process at a time, in under a week. No big system overhaul. Just one leak sealed. Want me to spot which one is costing you the most?

**When to use:** Prospect runs a service business (agency, consulting firm, professional services) where slow admin directly impacts cash flow. Good for businesses that invoice clients or manage recurring contracts.

---

### Script T1B-4: The Competitor Framing
*Angle: Others in their space are already automating — they're falling behind*

> [Name], the [sector] businesses I'm working with are automating their client onboarding, reporting, and follow-up sequences right now. The ones who aren't will feel it in 12 months. I'm not talking about overhauling your stack — just finding the one process eating the most hours and fixing it in a week. Would it be useful to see what that looks like in a business like yours?

**When to use:** Prospect is in a competitive sector (logistics, e-commerce, fintech, professional services). Use when you want to create urgency without being alarmist.

---

### Script T1B-5: The Specific Observation
*Angle: You've noticed something specific in their content that signals a bottleneck*

> [Name], I saw your post about [specific thing — e.g. "the manual reporting your team does every Monday"]. That's exactly the kind of process that seems small until you add it up across 50 weeks. I build automations for exactly that — one thing, done properly, in under a week. Most clients get 8-15 hours a week back from a single workflow fix. Worth a quick conversation?

**When to use:** Prospect has publicly posted or commented about a manual process, operational challenge, or team inefficiency. This is the most personalised version — always try to use this when you have a specific hook.

**After this:** Mark as **Cold** in the Lead Tracker. Do not send a fourth message.
